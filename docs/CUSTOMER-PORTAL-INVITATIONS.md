# Customer portal invitations and quoted products

From **Klanten → Stuur uitnodigingsmail**, choose Dutch, English or German (the saved customer language is preselected), confirm the recipient and download the `.eml` draft. The same dialog is available from portal access management. The invitation embeds the rAlabaster logo and includes a language-specific portal login link, the recipient's login address and clear instructions to use **Wachtwoord instellen of vergeten** to choose a password. Outlook opens it as an unsent multipart plain/HTML draft; no From address is imposed. The operator sends it manually.

The authenticated `portal-admin` action `prepare_invitation` provisions and links customer access without sending an invitation or password reset email. Only a planner administrator may call it. It rejects reassignment of another customer's address, planner-admin addresses and blocked customer memberships. Password setup uses the existing recovery flow when the recipient requests it. Choosing the mail language updates the customer's portal-language preference. Old invitation clients remain compatible.

Saved quotations now synchronize customer products and visible quantity prices in Supabase. Existing quotations are backfilled. Customer identity and template identity/name link products; the latest quotation wins, legacy name-only lines are resolved to template-linked lines, and unchanged quote data does not overwrite manual adjustments. Deliberately disabled products remain disabled. Test quotations, invalid/unpriced drafts, internal costing, margins, project notes and hidden price tiers are excluded. Discounts/surcharges are included in the customer price. New quotation lines retain their selected product-template ID.

The product page and order picker display visible quantity prices. The cart uses the applicable quantity threshold and updates totals without losing reference/date drafts. `portal_place_order` selects the price again on the server from the customer's active product; it never trusts a client-supplied unit price. Existing order prices are not rewritten.

Validation: `portal-invitation.test.cjs`, `portal-invitation-edge.test.cjs` and `portal-pricing.test.cjs` cover draft MIME, translations, logo, injection protection, access setup, customer actions and cart recalculation. `portal-quote-products-db.sql` tests automatic linking, legacy/template deduplication, latest prices, hidden tiers, discounts, repeat saves, invalid quantities, customer isolation and preservation of disabled products in an explicit rollback.
